45 Hard support

45 Hard is a 45-day all-or-nothing habit challenge for iPhone. Miss a goal on any day and the attempt ends; a new one starts the next day. Do it alone, or with friends who can follow your progress and cheer you on.

Need help?

Email contact@hasplabs.com. Say which iPhone and which version of the app you have, and what you expected to happen. We usually answer within a few days.

Challenges and goals

Create a challenge, pick its goals, and log each one before the day ends. A day is your local date, taken from your phone's time zone, and you can still log yesterday until 3:00 in the morning. Goals lock when a challenge starts. Water, workout and reading are logged by hand; a steps goal reads your step count from Apple Health once you allow it; a progress photo goal asks for one photo a day.

The server decides whether a day passed, once an hour, so a day may show its result a little after it ends. If a day was marked failed and you believe it was logged in time, email us with the date and we will look.

Steps and Apple Health

The steps goal reads only your daily step count, and never writes to Health. If steps are not being counted, open the Health app, go to Sharing, Apps, 45 Hard, and turn Steps on. If Health has no step data for the day, the goal stays unmet until it does.

Friends and shared challenges

Add a friend by searching for their handle on the Friends tab and sending a request. Once you are friends you can invite each other into a challenge; everyone in a shared challenge sees the same board: each participant's days, standings and feed. Cheer a friend from the feed.

Sharing and privacy settings

Profile, then Sharing. Five settings decide what other people see of you outside a shared challenge: Challenges, Calendar, Today's goals and History (each Nobody, Friends or Everyone) and Progress photos (Off or Friends). Everything starts at Friends with photos off. Public profile is off by default; while it is off, Everyone behaves like Friends and strangers can find only your handle and name. A shared challenge's board is not affected by these settings.

Progress photos

Your photos are private. A friend can see today's photo only if you turn Progress photos on and that friend can also see your Today's goals; past days are never shown to anyone but you. Friends receive a link that expires after five minutes. Retake a photo from today's log to replace it.

Blocking and reporting

Open a person's screen and tap the three-dot button. Block removes the friendship, hides you from each other, refuses requests and cheers both ways, and stops notifications between you; the person is not told. Report sends the profile, or a photo from its viewer, to us with a reason and an optional note. We read every report and act on it, which can include removing a photo or closing an account.

Notifications

Turn categories on or off under Profile, then Settings. To stop all notifications, turn them off for 45 Hard in iOS Settings. If you never see any, check that the app was allowed to send them when it first asked.

Your account and your data

Read the privacy policy for what the app stores and who can see it. To delete your account and everything in it, email contact@hasplabs.com from the address on your account; we confirm when it is done. Signing out does not delete anything.

Testing the app

45 Hard is currently available to invited testers through TestFlight. If a TestFlight build has expired or will not install, ask us for a new invitation.
